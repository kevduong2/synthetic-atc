01 · The idea

Why this project exists, in one minute

ASR models collapse on ATC radio: a zero-shot whisper-tiny.en reads real controller audio at 133% word error rate — worse than useless. Real labeled ATC audio is scarce and expensive to transcribe. So instead of collecting more of it, atc-gan manufactures it: scripts that are correct by construction, voices from TTS, and a radio channel simulated faithfully enough that a student model can't tell the difference where it matters.

It works. On a locked real-audio test slice never touched during development, the full recipe (75% real / 25% gated synthetic, then GRPO) reads at 20.35% WER against 22.40% for the same model trained on real data alone — a significant 2.06-point gain (95% CI [0.32, 4.23], paired bootstrap p=0.012). Details in section 08.

02 · Walkthrough

Follow one transmission through the pipe

The clearest way to see the architecture is to ride along with a single clip, from random seed to training batch.

  1. The grammar drafts a transmission — with its ground truth attached

    atcgen/text/grammar.py draws a scenario (region EU here, with knobs for readback errors, confusable callsigns, phonetic respelling) and emits three things at once: the spoken text the TTS will read, the display form, and a structured entity record. Nothing is ever parsed back out of the text — the label is born correct.

  2. Kokoro reads it, cleanly and on purpose

    The TTS stage (atcgen/tts/) is deliberately boring: Kokoro (Apache-2.0, ~82M params) behind an engine-agnostic synthesize(text, rng) → waveform; sample_rate = 24 kHz protocol, with a curated voice pool and rate perturbation for fast controller delivery. Realism is not its job — the research finding is that channel irregularity closes more of the real↔synthetic gap than TTS naturalness.

  3. The channel twin wrecks it in physical order

    atcgen/channel/chain.py runs the clip through mic coloration and PTT truncation once (the talker), then bandpass, fading, dropouts and noise per hop (the path), then co-channel mixing, AGC pumping and squelch at the receiver — with the passband re-applied where a real receiver would re-impose it. Every drawn parameter is recorded in a ChannelRecord; loudness uses a per-clip RMS target at 16 kHz; EBU R128 is available in channel/loudness.py. Full structure in section 06.

  4. Two frozen teachers decide whether the label survived

    The gate (atcgen/gate/) plays the degraded waveform to whisper-base.en and wav2vec2-base-960h — models that are not the student and, between them, not even the same architecture family. For our clip, both recover one two zero and the callsign; the words around them come back rough (best-teacher WER 0.31). All three critical slots verified, WER within the silver band → silver. Had every teacher heard a different flight level, the clip would be rejected outright — never relabeled to what the teachers heard.

  5. Tier selection builds the training buffer

    select_tiers admits gold and silver freely and caps adversarial clips — words crushed but safety numbers provable — at 5% of any mix. Rejected rows stay in the manifest with their reasons and full lineage (config hash, seed, profile, channel draw, teacher verdicts) but never train anything.

  6. The clip joins a 75/25 real/synthetic mix and trains the student

    training/recipe.py fine-tunes whisper-tiny.en on the mix (SFT), then GRPO sharpens it against transcription-quality rewards (section 09). Per-slice eval errors flow back to the steering layer, which decides what the next batch of clips should look like — closing the loop without ever touching a label or a weight from the outside.

03 · Flight plan

One pass around the loop

The same journey, seen from above. Five generation stages produce candidate training audio; the learning side consumes it next to real audio and reports per-slice errors; two steering loops close the circuit by choosing what to generate next. Nothing trains on a sample the gate did not pass, and nothing upstream ever touches the locked test split.

Three RL loops, kept apart on purpose

The research proposal's central design rule: RL appears at three separate points with separate rewards, because collapsing them into one adversarial loop produces label corruption and reward hacking. The PoC implements two of the three honestly and defers the third.

LoopOptimizesHereStatus
L1The speech generator itself (flow-matching GRPO) Deferred — needs the 5080 + license-cleared FM-TTS weights. Kokoro + DSP stands in. deferred
L2The ASR student training/grpo.py — GRPO on hypothesis groups, reward −WER with hallucination / repetition / length penalties, KL to the SFT checkpoint. Validated: −2.47 abs WER over SFT (p≈0). live
L3Which synthetic recipe to generate next atcgen/rl/bandit.py — Thompson-sampling bandit over recipes, bounded by the teacher-trust hardness window; audited by counterfactual runs. Audited: loses to uniform at PoC scale (§11). live

04 · Scenario service

Text with ground truth attached

A transcript is never a flat string. atcgen/text/grammar.py emits ICAO phraseology (EU and US variants) together with a structured entity record, a display form (BAW462) and the spoken form the TTS actually reads (speedbird four six two). An LLM may propose variation later, but a deterministic validator confirms callsign identity, value/unit relations, and legal ranges before anything is emitted — the generator cannot invent unvalidated transcripts.

  • atcgen/entities.py is the shared contract consumed by the grammar, the gate, and evaluation: Entity(type, value, spoken, critical) over callsign, runway, heading, altitude, flight level, frequency, speed, squawk, altimeter, waypoint, atis. Numeric safety entities carry critical=True.
  • Bidirectional spoken↔canonical digit conversion, including niner / tree / fife / fower, decimals, grouped forms (“one twenty seven”), ordinal runways (“26th center” → 26C), and join-insensitive telephony (“aero mexico” == “aeromexico”). Grammar output needs no parsing; the parser exists for teacher and student hypotheses and for real transcripts, where it fires on 72.7% of the jacktol corpus.
  • Vocabulary (110 airlines, 17 stations, 30 waypoints) is harvested from the jacktol train split only (scripts/harvest_vocab.py) — the real-data anchor without leaking eval vocabulary.
  • Text sources are pluggable (atcgen/text/sources.py): grammar:region=eu, local corpora, etc.

05 · Speech

Clean voices, deliberately boring

The speech stage is intentionally the least clever part: Kokoro TTS (Apache-2.0, ~82M params, fast on CPU/MPS) behind an engine-agnostic protocol — synthesize(text, rng) → waveform; sample_rate = 24 kHz — with a curated pool of adult US/UK voices and rate perturbation approximating fast controller delivery (atcgen/tts/). Realism is the channel's job: the research finding is that acoustic irregularity closes more of the real↔synthetic gap than TTS naturalness, so polish budget goes to the channel twin.

The second speech branch the proposal mandates — voice/accent conversion of real clips, the strongest branch in the published ATC study (synthetic-only 24.2% vs 33.8% WER with vs without it) — is deferred to a follow-up wave (kNN-VC toward real ATC speakers). The protocol interface is where it will slot in, and it is the leading candidate for closing the remaining synthetic-only gap (§08).

One documented wrinkle: Kokoro is nondeterministic on MPS (~−19 dB rendering noise at identical seeds), which weakens common-random-numbers pairing in the RL harness (docs/known-issues.md).

06 · Radio-channel twin

A channel with physical ordering

atcgen/channel/chain.py runs a config-declared chain of DSP primitives with per-sample randomness: each step fires with probability prob and draws every parameter from its declared distribution; ChannelRecord keeps what was actually applied, for the manifest. The chain is organized by where each effect physically happens, not by config convenience:

Two operating modes, plus a learned residual

  • Mode 1 — procedural. The chain above with a capped domain-randomization envelope: distortion ranges are bounded by statistics measured from real recordings (unlimited randomization manufactures mislabeled data). Profiles in configs/mode1_*.yaml.
  • Mode 2 — per-clip calibrated. atcgen/channel/learned/ fits channel parameters to individual real calibration clips (own SDR captures under data/real/calibration/) and replays matched presets.
  • CUT residual (optional). A small contrastive translation model (learned/residual.py) adds only what the DSP misses; the DSP stays auditable and versioned underneath. Training it is 5080 work.
  • Noise beds come from real recordings (atcgen/dataset/noise_harvest.py); loudness uses a per-clip RMS target; EBU R128 is available in channel/loudness.py.

07 · Verification gate

Does the audio provably say what the label claims?

Every candidate sample faces a panel of frozen teachers — whisper-base.en (encoder-decoder, language-modelled) and wav2vec2-base-960h (CTC, frame-synchronous, no decoder LM; the architecturally distinct one) — after the channel, on the exact waveform the student would train on. The verdict is reject, never relabel: a sample whose label the panel cannot confirm is dropped, not rewritten to whatever the teachers heard, because relabelling would launder generator errors into ground truth.

08 · Student training + the experiment matrix

SFT → mix → GRPO, budget-matched

training/recipe.py is glue, not a framework: every SFT arm runs the same 2,000 fixed optimizer steps (reusing atcgen.rl.finetune_lite — seeded, no HF Trainer), so arms differ in what the student sees, never in how much gradient it gets. Fine-tuning is full — PEFT LoRA is structurally incompatible with Whisper. A4 then adds a 600-step GRPO stage on top of the A3 checkpoint, so the A4−A3 comparison isolates exactly what GRPO buys.

09 · GRPO mechanics

How the student sharpens itself

SFT teaches the student what ATC audio sounds like; GRPO teaches it to stop guessing. For each training clip the model samples a group of transcripts at temperature, scores each with a reward it cannot game, and reinforces whatever beat its own group's average — anchored by a KL term so it never drifts far from the SFT checkpoint.

10 · Evaluation + data discipline

Entity metrics gate releases, not aggregate WER

In ATC, command accuracy and WER decouple — one wrong digit is operationally serious while five garbled filler words are not. atcgen/eval/entity_metrics.py therefore reports, per arm and per slice: WER with S/D/I decomposition, exact callsign accuracy, per-type slot F1, the critical-number substitution rate, and hallucination metrics. Text normalization is frozen in training/normalize.py so arms stay comparable, and training/evaluate.py --hyps-out writes per-utterance hypotheses so any two arms can be paired-bootstrapped after the fact.

Generator-fidelity tiers

Separately from student metrics, atcgen/eval/harness.py regression-tests the generator: tier 0 generation stats, tier 1 channel statistics (LTAS and friends) against real references, then embedding distance and a real-vs-synthetic probe on frozen WavLM features. At the current reference size the probe is saturated upward — it reads 0.98–1.00 for everything, clean TTS included — so it cannot rank channel work until a ~1k labeled real set arrives. KID on speech embeddings (plus LTAS L1) is the working fidelity headline, tracked as a trend rather than a release gate; calibrated-mode numbers are additionally in-sample for the channel fit (see docs/known-issues.md).

11 · Steering

Choosing what to generate next

Two loops sit above the pipeline. The outer config loop (atcgen/rl/loop.py, CEM with a random-search control) tunes generator configuration against a whisper-tiny WER reward, built for iterations that cost most of an hour: crash-idempotent (trials.jsonl + optimizer checkpoints, restart resumes at the next un-evaluated trial), failed candidates dropped from the batch rather than fed as sentinel rewards, and the hand-tuned config seeded as trial 0 so every reward reads against the thing to beat. (Its runs/rl_v1 best config predates the channel-physics re-fit and needs a re-run.)

The L3 recipe bandit (atcgen/rl/bandit.py) decides which of 12 recipe buckets — scenario class, voice, rate, SNR band, channel condition, difficulty — to generate from next. Per-action retraining is impossible, so each pull scores its batch with a cheap proxy:

12 · Limitations section

Invariants — violations block merge

  1. Judge/teacher ASR ≠ student ASR; teachers frozen, at least one architecturally distinct (here: wav2vec2 CTC vs. the whisper family).
  2. No sample trains or rewards anything without passing the gate; failed samples are rejected, never relabeled.
  3. Adversarial tier ≤ 5% of any training mix.
  4. Student-hardness signals never reach a generator objective; hardness lives in L3, bounded by WER_teacher < τ₁.
  5. Real splits are mutually disjoint and disjoint from all upstream fitting; locked_test is read once per arm.
  6. Synthetic data is always evaluated mixed with real replay, never alone, when the question is “does it help the student” — the replay-corrected counterfactual exists because of this rule.
  7. Releases gate on the entity/safety panel, never on aggregate WER alone.
  8. No LiveATC audio in any training path; no CC-BY-NC checkpoints in the product.
  9. Every dataset row carries lineage: config hash, seed, profile, text source, channel parameters, gate verdicts.

Deferred items

PoC deviations, and the queue behind them

Deliberate, documented, and honest about hardware and licensing — deferred, not stubbed:

  • Generator class (L1). Kokoro + DSP/CUT stands in for flow-matching TTS with generator GRPO; FM training needs the 5080 and license-cleared pretraining data (public F5-TTS weights are CC-BY-NC).
  • VC/accent branch. Deferred; the biggest published lever for synthetic-only parity, and the top candidate for the next round.
  • Judge diversity + strength. Two teachers instead of a Canary-class panel. Callsign verification is currently teacher-bounded (~0.16 recall on degraded audio), not parser-bounded — stronger teachers directly raise gold yield.
  • Stale outer-loop config. runs/rl_v1's best config predates the channel-physics re-fit; the CEM search needs a re-run before its numbers are quotable.
  • Split leakage. jacktol's utterance segmentation permits speaker/callsign overlap across train/test; accepted for the PoC, prohibited in production, replaced by the user's own transcribed set later.
